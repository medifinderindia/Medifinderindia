What is MediFinder India?

MediFinder India is a platform that connects customers with licensed medicine shops, pharmacies, healthcare service providers and delivery partners. We do not operate our own medicine shop or pharmacy. Instead, we bring legally licensed and verified medicine shops onto our platform so that they can reach more customers and provide faster service.

Our Mission — We want to reduce the time people spend searching for medicines and healthcare services, while helping local licensed pharmacies and healthcare providers grow their businesses.
Helping Local Pharmacies Grow

Many medicine shops already have the medicines customers need, but customers may not know which shop has it, whether it's available, or how quickly it can reach them. MediFinder India solves this connectivity problem — helping participating pharmacies reach more customers, increase sales opportunities and build a stronger digital presence.

Fast Delivery, With Responsibility

Speed is important in healthcare, but it must never come at the cost of safety or legal compliance. For prescription-controlled medicines, the relevant licensed pharmacy is responsible for verifying the prescription before dispensing. Our delivery network transports medicines and other permitted products securely and responsibly.

We Follow the Law

MediFinder India does not support illegal medicine sales, fake or fraudulent prescriptions, unauthorized pharmaceutical distribution, counterfeit medicines, medicine tampering, illegal drug trafficking, or any other unlawful activity. We work only with licensed and legally authorized pharmacies and service providers.

Our Vision

To build a trusted healthcare-access network where people can discover Medicine + Pharmacy + Lab Test + Healthcare Instrument + Baby Care + Nurse + Ambulance + other healthcare services from one convenient digital platform, subject to availability in their location.

How We Verify Pharmacies

Before a pharmacy is listed on MediFinder India, we check:

  • A valid Drug Licence and GSTIN
  • Registered pharmacist details
  • Shop address and contact details

Prescription medicines are dispensed only after the pharmacy verifies a valid prescription.
