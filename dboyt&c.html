2. Data Collection Framework Concerning Delivery Partners

To establish a verified, safe, and lightning-fast logistics framework for medical packages, we collect highly granular technical and personal identifiers from our independent Delivery Partners:

A. Onboarding and Regulatory Vetting Data

Full legal name, background verification declarations, permanent residential addresses, active WhatsApp/mobile numbers, emergency contact metrics, and passport-size photographs. To comply with transport mandates, we ingest scanned images of the vehicle’s Driving License, permanent account number (PAN), Aadhaar Card details, Vehicle Registration Certificates (RC Book), and third-party commercial vehicle insurance matrices.

B. Real-Time Geolocation Infrastructure (Foreground and Background Tracking)

To accurately calculate the Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA), match orders efficiently, and preserve the temperature control tracking of medical packages, our application gathers precise, real-time global positioning system (GPS) coordinates (latitude, longitude, and altitude). This tracking operates continuously in the foreground and background of your operating system environment even when the application icon is closed or actively unused, from the moment a delivery partner logs in as "Active" until they execute an explicit system log-out.

C. Financial and Payout Identifiers

Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and Unified Payments Interface (UPI) parameters needed to calculate and disburse weekly delivery fees, long-distance incentives, surge adjustments, and customer tips.

3. Data Collection Regarding Customers and Sellers

  • Customer Data: Profile registries, active delivery locations, text inquiries regarding medicine availability, and digital copies of medical prescriptions required for validating Schedule H/X pharmaceutical allocations.
  • Seller Data: Commercial drug distribution licenses, pharmacist certifications, store addresses, inventory stock volumes, maximum retail prices (MRP), and integrated PMS/POS ledger configurations.

4. Advanced Purposes of Data Processing

We process collected parameters strictly for the execution of legitimate business systems:

  1. Algorithmic Logistics Coordination: Matching an active customer medicine request to the nearest pharmacy holding matching stock, and assigning the closest active Delivery Partner to coordinate instantaneous pick-up.
  2. Public Safety & Anti-Fraud Vetting: Validating that medical packets are handled only by background-verified personnel to mitigate the risks of chemical product tampering, illegal substance diversions, or transit theft.
  3. Transactional Communications: Utilizing automated gateways to dispatch real-time alerts via SMS, encrypted emails, and automated WhatsApp packets regarding delivery milestones and dispatch status.
Data Localization Standard: In harmony with statutory requirements, Medi Finder India securely saves all data records within localized cloud frameworks inside the territorial boundaries of India, deploying strict access control layers.

5. Information Sharing, Disclosure, and Intermediary Routing

We do not trade, monetize, or lease personal records to third-party ad brokers. Data routing occurs strictly under these structural scenarios:

  • Transit Disclosures: The designated Delivery Partner receives access to the customer's name, masked telephone metric, and precise delivery address map to safely execute the logistical route. Concurrently, the Customer is shown the assigned Delivery Partner's real-time GPS location, name, and photograph.
  • Statutory Drug Enforcement Demands: If demanded by State Drug Controllers, Law Enforcement Authorities, or Judicial Subpoenas under the Information Technology Act, we will render specific transaction histories, prescription logs, or rider route records without user notification.

6. Comprehensive Security Safeguards

Our infrastructure deploys advanced administrative, physical, and digital safeguards. All network communications are wrapped in Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS 1.3) data encryption protocols. Financial checkouts are handled strictly via PCI-DSS compliant secure processing nodes. While we exert highest efforts to safeguard account spaces, users must protect their local access devices, password integrity, and OTP flows from unauthorized third-party eyes.

7. Data Retention, Erasure, and Account Erasure Architecture

We retain parameters only for the duration required to satisfy our core service operations or as demanded by Indian statutory tracking laws.
