Delivery Partner Policy

MediFinder - Terms & Conditions for Delivery Partners

1. Onboarding

  • Valid driving license and vehicle registration required
  • KYC verification must be completed before first delivery
  • Vehicle must be in good condition

2. Duty & Availability

  • Toggle "On Duty" only when available for deliveries
  • Maintain minimum 10 active hours per week for payout eligibility
  • Keep phone charged and app running during duty

3. Order Handling

  • Accept orders promptly when available
  • Follow the navigation route provided
  • Collect OTP from customer before marking delivered
  • Handle medicines with care â€” they are temperature-sensitive

4. Payouts

  • Earnings are calculated per delivery (base + distance bonus)
  • Minimum 10 active hours required for payout request
  • Payouts are processed within 24-48 hours of request
  • 10% platform commission is deducted

5. Conduct

  • Be polite and professional with customers
  • Do not open or tamper with medicine packages
  • Maintain vehicle hygiene
  • Follow traffic rules at all times

6. Suspension

Accounts may be suspended for: repeated order cancellations, late deliveries, customer complaints, tampering with packages, or fraudulent activity.
