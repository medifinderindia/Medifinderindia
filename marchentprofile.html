Quick FAQ Guide

Fast answers to the questions merchants ask most.


How do I add a new product?

Tap the red ADD button in the bottom navigation bar. Fill in the product details across the three steps — Basics, Info, and Assets — then tap Upload Product. It goes live once admin approves it (usually shown as "Pending" until then).

How do I ship an accepted order?

Open the Orders page from the bottom navigation. Accepted orders appear there with the customer's details — pack the items and hand them to your regular delivery partner or rider. Mark the order as Shipped once it's out for delivery.

How do I handle a prescription order?

When a customer nearby needs a prescription medicine, you'll get a request in the Prescription panel on the Home page (tap the prescription icon in the header). Review the uploaded prescription image, then Accept or Decline — accepting reserves it for you and reduces stock automatically.

Why can't I publish products yet?

Your government drug license needs to be verified first. Upload it from Profile → Government License Hub. Product publishing unlocks automatically once an admin marks it Verified.

How do I get my license or bank details verified?

Open Government License Hub or Bank Account Details (KYC) from Profile, fill in the details, and upload the document image, then save. Your submission shows as Pending Review — an admin checks it and marks it Verified (or Rejected, in which case you can re-upload). While Pending, you can still Edit or Cancel the submission from the same card.
