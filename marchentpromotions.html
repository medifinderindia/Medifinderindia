How Promotions Work
Every offer you create here — a Discount, Coupon, or Flash Sale — follows the same 3-step journey before it reaches a customer.
1. You Create It
Set the type, value, and dates in Discounts, Coupons, or Flash Sales.
2. Attach Products
Tap products in the grid to link the offer to exactly those items.
3. Customer Sees It
The offer shows automatically on those products in the customer app.
Discount Types Explained
Discount %
Cuts the price by a percentage. Great for wide sales.
e.g. 20% off → ₹500 becomes ₹400
Flat Discount
Cuts a fixed rupee amount off, regardless of price.
e.g. ₹50 off → ₹500 becomes ₹450
Buy X Get Y
Customer buys a set quantity and gets more free or discounted.
e.g. Buy 1 Get 1 free
Minimum Purchase
Offer only unlocks once the cart crosses this amount.
e.g. Min order ₹300 to apply
Expiry Date
Every offer needs an end date. Once that date passes, the offer automatically stops working for customers — it flips to Expired here in your dashboard and won't apply at checkout anymore. You don't need to remove it manually.
