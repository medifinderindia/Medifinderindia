Privacy Policy (Merchant)

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Welcome to MediFinder India (accessible via medifinderindia.com, hereinafter referred to as the "Platform", "we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including medicine shops, sellers, registered pharmacists (collectively referred to as "Merchant Partners", "Sellers", or "Shops"), and customers who utilize our platform to search for and verify the availability of medicines.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access our website, mobile applications, and online inventory management features (collectively, the "Services"). By registering an account, integrating your inventory, or using our Services, you explicitly consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Applicability and Scope

This policy applies globally but is structured in strict compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India. It covers:

  • Merchant Partners & Sellers: Pharmacy owners, retail medical stores, whole-sellers, and their authorized personnel who list medicine stock on our Platform.
  • End Users / Customers: Individuals looking for medicine availability, location-based pharmacy discovery, and fast delivery options.
  • Digital Visitors: Anyone browsing medifinderindia.com or interacting with our communication channels, emails, and support logs.

2. Information We Collect from Medicine Shops & Sellers

To list your shop and update medicine availability on MediFinder India, we collect specific commercial, professional, and personal indicators. This data ensures legitimacy and regulatory compliance with Indian pharmaceutical laws:

A. Onboarding & Registration Data

  • Full Name, professional email address, and active mobile numbers of shop owners or managers.
  • Official Store Name, registered commercial address, and geographical coordinates (GPS) for location-based searches.
  • Valid Drug License copies, Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN), and Pharmacist Registration Certificates.

B. Inventory and Stock Records

  • Medicine names, formulations, batch numbers, stock availability levels, manufacturing details, and maximum retail prices (MRP).
  • Real-time product availability changes updated manually or via integrated Pharmacy Management Systems (PMS) or Point of Sale (POS) software APIs.

C. Financial & Transactional Data

  • Bank account details, UPI IDs, and billing statements necessary for processing subscription fees, platform payouts, or handling customer prepayments.

3. Information We Collect from Customers / Buyers

To connect consumers with nearby medicine shops efficiently, we process the following information:

  • Account Profiles: Name, contact numbers, email addresses, and delivery coordinates.
  • Search Intent & Inquiries: Particular medicine search terms, uploaded images of prescriptions, and communication logs with specific shops.
  • Precise Location Data: Continuous or real-time GPS locations to calculate the exact distance between the buyer and the nearest active pharmacy.

4. How We Use the Collected Data

We utilize the data collected from both Sellers and Customers to maintain a high-performance inventory search network. Specific operations include:

  • Core Service Matching: Showing customer search requests to the closest relevant medicine sellers matching the required stock.
  • Inventory Accuracy: Refining platform algorithms to reflect accurate stock data and preventing ghost listings of critical medicines.
  • Verification and Legality: Ensuring that all registered sellers hold active, valid drug distribution licenses to prevent illegal pharmaceutical listings.
  • Communication Channels: Facilitating rapid automated order updates via SMS, email, and WhatsApp notifications regarding medicine status.

5. Data Sharing and Commercial Protections

We do not sell user data to third-party data brokers. Data sharing is limited to the following essential technical and operational scenarios:

  • Customer-Seller Bridging: Once a medicine's availability is confirmed, the customer's delivery address and phone number are shared with the respective medicine shop to execute rapid delivery.
  • Delivery/Logistics Partners: Sharing essential delivery routing details with internal or third-party delivery agents associated with MediFinder India.
  • Regulatory & Legal Mandates: We may share detailed transaction records, stock records, or user identities if demanded by state or central drug controller authorities, law enforcement agencies, or under explicit court orders.
Government Identifier Protection Policy: MediFinder India safely handles all regulatory data required for vendor vetting. For transparency, we meticulously redact or isolate critical personal identifiers within our storage infrastructure. We do not store or display sensitive government indicators beyond strict validation requirements.

6. Automatic Data Collection Technologies

Our web servers automatically track browsing behaviors via technical electronic tools, including:

  • Log Files & Device Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, unique mobile device IDs, and internet connection speeds.
  • Cookies & Tracking Pixels: Small text files deployed to save session configurations, shop dashboard settings, and tracking active product searches. You can control cookie preferences directly inside your web browser settings.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your store or customer account remains active on medifinderindia.com.

  • Deletion Requests: Sellers and buyers can request permanent deletion of their account records at any time. Upon verified request, we will purge active records from our live production databases.
  • Backups & Archival Logs: Residual data copies may persist in encrypted off-site backup servers for a legal buffer period to fulfill auditing requirements, resolve financial disputes, or prevent recurring fraudulent platform behavior.

8. Comprehensive Security Standards

We employ enterprise-grade administrative, physical, and technological security controls to safe-keep your data. All communication between your pharmacy dashboard and our main cloud servers is fully secured using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) encryption technology. Payment transactions are executed using standard PCI-DSS compliant secure gateways. However, no data transmission mechanism over the open internet can be guaranteed as 100% impenetrable; therefore, users are strictly advised to protect account credentials and API keys.

9. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to alter, modify, or rewrite this Privacy Policy at our discretion to align with changing digital regulations or expanded feature rollouts. The "Last Updated" date at the absolute top of this page will always indicate the newest revision date. Your continued utilization of our services after updates are published implies absolute acknowledgment of the modified guidelines.
