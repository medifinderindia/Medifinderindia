Merchant Policy

MediFinder - Terms & Conditions for Pharmacies

1. Verification

Valid pharmacy license (Drug License) is mandatory. Accounts remain in "Pending" status until admin approval. Upload clear, readable copies of your license.

2. Product Listing

  • All medicines must be genuine and within expiry date
  • Prices must comply with MRP regulations
  • Product images must be accurate
  • Rx medicines must be verified against uploaded prescriptions

3. Order Fulfillment

  • Accept orders within 15 minutes of notification
  • Prepare and pack medicines securely
  • Hand over to delivery partner with all items verified
  • Update order status in real-time

4. Payments

Payouts are processed weekly. Platform commission is deducted automatically. Merchant can request early payout via the payment section.

5. Quality Assurance

MediFinder conducts random quality checks. Merchants found selling expired or counterfeit medicines will be permanently banned and reported to authorities.

6. Suspension

Accounts may be suspended for: late order fulfillment, poor quality medicines, customer complaints, or violation of platform policies.
