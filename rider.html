Frequently Asked Questions

How do I accept a delivery order?
Turn on 'On Duty' from the Home page. New orders will appear automatically — tap 'Accept Request' on any order card to accept it. Your Driving License must be verified first, otherwise the Accept button stays locked.
How do I complete a delivery?
Go to the Delivery tab, follow the live map to the customer, then tap the timer/OTP card and enter the 6-digit code given by the customer to mark it delivered.
How do I request a payout?
Open the Earning tab and tap 'Request Payout to Admin' on the Total Earnings card. Make sure your Bank & Payment details are verified in Profile first, or the payout may be delayed.
How do I complete my KYC (Vehicle, License, Identity, Bank)?
Go to Profile and tap 'Verify your account to earn money'. Fill in the 5 steps (Rider Details, Vehicle, License & Insurance, Identity, Bank) and submit. Status will show Pending Review (yellow) until admin checks it, then Verified (green) or Rejected (red) with a reason and a chance to fix and resubmit.
A notification won't go away — how do I remove it?
Open the bell icon on Home, then tap the ✕ on any notification to remove it permanently. Notifications also auto-remove after 10 days, and order notifications disappear automatically once another rider accepts that order.
