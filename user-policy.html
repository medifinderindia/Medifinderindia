User Policy

MediFinder - Terms & Conditions for Users

1. Account Registration

You must provide accurate information during signup. One account per user. MediFinder reserves the right to suspend accounts with fake or misleading information.

2. Prescription Medicines

Rx medicines require a valid prescription uploaded before purchase. Fake or tampered prescriptions will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to authorities.

3. Orders & Payments

  • Orders once placed cannot be modified
  • Cancellation is allowed before the order is shipped
  • COD orders must be paid in exact change
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days

4. Delivery

  • Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees
  • Please provide accurate address and phone number
  • Share the delivery OTP only with the delivery partner

5. Privacy

Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Prohibited Activities

  • Ordering medicines for resale
  • Sharing account credentials
  • Abusing the return/refund system
  • Harassing delivery partners
