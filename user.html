How to order medicine

Search or browse a category on the home page, add items to your cart, confirm your delivery address, and place the order. You'll get a live order status in My Orders.

How to upload a prescription

On the home page, tap Camera to click a photo of your prescription or Gallery / PDF to upload an existing file. It's verified automatically and linked to your Rx orders.

How to track my order

Go to My Orders and tap Track on any active order to see live delivery status and location on the map.

How to cancel an order

Open My Orders, select the order, and tap Cancel. Orders already out for delivery may not be cancellable.

Refund policy

Refunds for cancelled or returned orders are processed back to your original payment method, typically within 5-7 business days.

Delivery charge

Delivery charges depend on your distance from the pharmacy and order value; the exact charge is shown on the checkout page before you pay.

Return policy

Damaged, expired, or incorrect items can be returned within 24 hours of delivery. Medicines cannot be returned once the seal is opened, except in case of a genuine error.

Payment failed but amount deducted

If a payment fails after the amount was deducted, it is automatically reversed to your account within 24-48 hours. Contact support if it isn't reversed after that window.
